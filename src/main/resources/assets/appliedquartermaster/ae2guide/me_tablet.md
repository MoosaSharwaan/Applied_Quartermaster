# ME Tablet

<ItemImage id="appliedquartermaster:me_tablet" scale="4" />

The ME Tablet runs your network from anywhere in range. Right-click to open it; sneak and right-click to always open the
**Modules** page.

## Modules

Open the **Modules** page with the gear at the top right and put a wireless terminal in a module slot: an
<ItemLink id="ae2:wireless_terminal" />, a <ItemLink id="ae2:wireless_crafting_terminal" /> or a universal terminal.
Each module gets a tab. Its tab opens that real AE2 terminal; press **Esc** there to come back to the tablet, and **Esc**
in the tablet to close it.

The tablet reaches the network the terminal module is linked to, through a powered
<ItemLink id="ae2:wireless_access_point" /> in range. Link the terminal first, as you normally would.

## Tabs that unlock

The **Library**, **Armory**, **Tools** and **Automation** tabs appear while their block is on the linked network.

* Click an item to pick it up (it swaps with the item you hold); shift-click to move it to your inventory.
* Shift-click an item in your inventory to store it on the network.
* The buttons on the left sort, show or hide free spots, and switch between Large, Medium and Small views.

## Default tab

Right-click any tab, or click the pin on a module, to make it the tab that opens first. The pinned tab has a gold pin.
