# ME Armory and ME Tool Rack

<Row gap="20">
<BlockImage id="appliedquartermaster:me_armory" scale="5" />
<BlockImage id="appliedquartermaster:me_tool_rack" scale="5" />
</Row>

The **ME Armory** holds 8 weapons and mining tools. The **ME Tool Rack** holds 8 utility tools such as wrenches and
memory cards. Each uses **1 channel**. Place several side by side or stacked, facing the same way, and their frames merge
into one cabinet.

Right-click a block to open its slots, or use the tablet's **Armory** and **Tools** tabs.
